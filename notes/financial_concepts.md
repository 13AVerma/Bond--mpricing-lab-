# Financial Concepts

## Bond

A bond is a promise to pay money in the future.

A fixed-rate bond pays fixed coupons on scheduled dates and repays principal at maturity.

## Coupon

The coupon rate determines the size of the interest payments.

Example:

Face amount = 1,000,000  
Coupon rate = 2%

Annual coupon = 20,000

If paid semiannually, each coupon is roughly 10,000.

## Schedule

The schedule determines when the coupon payments happen.

## Accrued Interest

Accrued interest is the part of the next coupon that has built up since the previous coupon date.

## Clean Price

Clean price is the quoted bond price excluding accrued interest.

## Dirty Price

Dirty price is the actual economic price including accrued interest.

Dirty price = clean price + accrued interest

## Yield Curve

A yield curve tells us how to discount future cashflows back to today.

The coupon creates the cashflows.

The yield curve values those cashflows today.

## Credit Spread

A credit spread is an extra yield added because the issuer is riskier than the risk-free curve.

Higher spread means higher discount rate.

Higher discount rate means lower bond price.
